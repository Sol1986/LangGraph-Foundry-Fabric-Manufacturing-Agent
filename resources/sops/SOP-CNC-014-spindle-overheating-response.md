# SOP-CNC-014: CNC Spindle Overheating Response

Applies to all CNC machines in Plants 1, 2 and 3.

## Symptoms
High spindle temperature alarm, automatic thermal shutdown, dimensional drift on finished parts, discoloured chips.

## Immediate response
1. Stop the cycle and let the spindle idle until the alarm clears.
2. Check coolant reservoir level, coolant flow at the nozzle, and the coolant filter.
3. Top up coolant only to the marked level. Record the amount added.
4. Run a test part and check critical dimensions before resuming production.

## Escalation
If a machine triggers two or more overheating events in one shift, or three or more in a week, stop using the machine and raise a corrective work order. Do not rely on topping up coolant as a repeat fix. Check the date of the last coolant system service against SOP-MNT-003.

## Quality note
Parts produced during or shortly after an overheating event should be inspected at 100 percent for dimensional tolerance until the machine is verified stable.
