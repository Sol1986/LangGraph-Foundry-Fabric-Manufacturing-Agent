# SOP-MNT-003: Preventive Maintenance Intervals

Maintenance intervals are measured from the last completed service date.

| Component | Interval |
|---|---|
| Lubrication (all machines) | 30 days |
| CNC coolant system (flush, filter, pump check, concentration test) | 90 days |
| Press hydraulic system | 90 days |
| Welder electrode assembly | 90 days |
| Assembly conveyor drive | 90 days |
| Packaging sealing unit | 90 days |

## Overdue service
A service is overdue when the current date is past last service date plus the interval. Overdue services on production machines should be scheduled within 14 days. Supervisors may defer a service only with a written work order and a planned completion date.

## Records
All completed services are logged in the maintenance system with date, component and technician.
